# RescueSync

RescueSync distinguishes public hazard information from organization-managed disaster response.

## Language

**External Hazard Event**:
A provider-identified record describing a natural hazard, alert, or event, with its original source and provenance.
Its existence does not establish an operational response inside RescueSync.
_Avoid_: Managed incident, response incident

**Managed Incident**:
An organization-owned response operation managed inside RescueSync, which may reference one or more external hazard events.
_Avoid_: External hazard event, provider alert

**Regional Hazard Triage**:
Assessment of reported hazards relevant to Malaysia and Southeast Asia to select events for closer investigation using their original sources.
_Avoid_: Comprehensive local warning coverage, personnel threat assessment

**Provider Representative Location**:
A point designated by a provider to represent a hazard event geographically, with positional accuracy and observation meaning limited to what the provider establishes.
_Avoid_: Current cyclone position, affected boundary, damage extent
